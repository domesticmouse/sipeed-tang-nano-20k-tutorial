# Course Working Notes & Curriculum Roadmap

## Learner Profile & Preferences
- **Background:** Strong high-level programming experience, past C experience, dabbled with microcontrollers (MCUs), and builds analog op-amp synthesizer circuits (TL074 buffered mults, envelope followers).
- **HDL Experience:** Knows HDLs exist, brand new to writing HDL code.
- **Learning Strategy:** Follow a paired **two-lesson cadence**: introduce each hardware concept in **Amaranth HDL** (`amaranth.lib.wiring.Component`) with `amaranth.sim` + `pytest` verification in an odd-numbered lesson, then dedicate the following even-numbered lesson to dissecting its generated Verilog line by line.
- **Hardware & Host:** Sipeed Tang Nano 20K (`GW2AR-LV18QN88C8/I7`) mounted on a breadboard; macOS Apple Silicon (`arm64`) using `uv` and OSS CAD Suite (`yosys`, `nextpnr-himbaechel`, `gowin_pack`, `openFPGALoader`).
- **Analogy Anchor:** Frame combinational (`m.d.comb`) vs synchronous (`m.d.sync`) logic around parallel continuous analog signal paths and sample-and-hold/clocked latches rather than sequential CPU loops.

## Curriculum Roadmap
- **Phase 1 — LEDs, Verilog Literacy & Buttons**
  - `0001-blinky-amaranth-to-verilog`: First Amaranth `wiring.Component`, `m.d.sync` vs `m.d.comb`, active-low LEDs, `pytest` simulation, `.cst` pin constraints, and flashing.
  - `0002-dissecting-generated-verilog`: Line-by-line breakdown of Yosys-generated `blinky.v` (escaped identifiers, `[MSB:LSB]` buses, carry-preserving widths, sized literals, `reg` vs `wire`, `always @(posedge clk)` `<=` vs `always @*` `=`, and `$auto$` sim guards).
  - `0003-buttons-and-debouncing`: Active-high buttons `S1`/`S2`, 2-flop metastability synchronizers, edge detection, and counter-based debouncing in Amaranth.
  - `0004-buttons-generated-verilog`: Dissecting the generated Verilog for `S1`/`S2` synchronizers, edge detectors, and debounce counters.
  - `0005-pwm-and-state-machines`: Pulse-width modulation (PWM) LED brightness control and Finite State Machines (FSMs) in Amaranth.
  - `0006-pwm-fsm-generated-verilog`: Dissecting how Amaranth lowers FSM states (`case`, one-hot/binary encoding) and PWM comparators into Verilog.
- **Phase 2 — USB-UART Serial**
  - `0007-uart-tx`: Baud-rate tick generator, shift registers, and streaming text from the FPGA to the macOS terminal via pin 69 in Amaranth.
  - `0008-uart-tx-generated-verilog`: Dissecting UART TX shift-register, bit-slice, and baud-counter Verilog.
  - `0009-uart-rx-and-echo`: Oversampling UART receiver (pin 70), framing, and interactive terminal control of onboard LEDs in Amaranth.
  - `0010-uart-rx-generated-verilog`: Dissecting oversampling RX state-machine and framing Verilog.
- **Phase 3 — HDMI Video**
  - `0011-pll-and-video-timing`: Gowin PLL clock multiplication (27 MHz → 125 MHz / 25 MHz) and 640×480 video sync/coordinate generator in Amaranth.
  - `0012-pll-video-timing-generated-verilog`: Dissecting black-box vendor `Instance` instantiation, multi-clock domains, and video timing counters in Verilog.
  - `0013-tmds-and-hdmi-output`: TMDS 8b/10b encoding, `OSER10` / `ELVDS_OBUF` primitives, and live HDMI color bars & test patterns in Amaranth.
  - `0014-tmds-hdmi-generated-verilog`: Dissecting TMDS combinational encoders and differential I/O primitive wiring in Verilog.
- **Phase 4 — Retro Gaming**
  - `0015-sprites-and-tilemaps`: Block RAM tilemaps, sprite blitting, and button/UART player input in Amaranth.
  - `0016-sprites-tilemaps-generated-verilog`: Dissecting inferred/explicit Gowin Block RAM (`Memory`) and sprite pipeline Verilog.
  - `0017-i2s-audio-and-playable-game`: MAX98357A I2S chiptune audio synthesis and a complete playable retro game core in Amaranth.
  - `0018-i2s-game-generated-verilog`: Dissecting the full multi-module retro-gaming core and I2S audio Verilog.
- **Phase 5 — Storage, SDRAM & Game Boy (DMG) Core**
  - `0019-spi-and-microsd-loading`: SPI master and reading raw ROM files from the onboard microSD slot in Amaranth.
  - `0020-spi-microsd-generated-verilog`: Dissecting SPI master state-machine, clock-divider, and shift-register Verilog.
  - `0021-sdram-controller`: Driving the GW2AR-18's integrated 64 Mbit SDRAM (refresh, banks, row/column timing) for cartridge ROMs and system memory in Amaranth.
  - `0022-sdram-generated-verilog`: Dissecting SDRAM controller FSM, refresh timing, and bidirectional bus Verilog.
  - `0023-gameboy-sm83-cpu`: SM83 CPU (ALU, register file, fetch/decode/execute FSM, interrupts) verified against `blargg`/`mooneye` CPU test ROMs in `pytest`.
  - `0024-gameboy-sm83-generated-verilog`: Dissecting SM83 CPU datapath, instruction decode tables, and control FSM Verilog.
  - `0025-gameboy-bus-timer-joypad-mbc`: Memory map, timers, 8-button joypad input, and MBC1/MBC3 cartridge bank switching in Amaranth.
  - `0026-gameboy-bus-mbc-generated-verilog`: Dissecting address decoding, timer dividers, and MBC bank-switching Verilog.
  - `0027-gameboy-ppu-to-hdmi`: 160×144 Game Boy PPU (background, window, 40 sprites, OAM DMA) and scanline upscaling to 640×480 HDMI in Amaranth.
  - `0028-gameboy-ppu-generated-verilog`: Dissecting PPU pixel pipeline, OAM DMA, and scanline-buffer Verilog.
  - `0029-gameboy-apu-and-integration`: 4-channel Game Boy APU (2 pulse, wave, noise → MAX98357A I2S) and full playable DMG core integration in Amaranth.
  - `0030-gameboy-full-core-generated-verilog`: Dissecting the 4-channel APU mixers and full integrated Game Boy DMG Verilog.
- **Phase 6 — Commodore 64 (C64) Core**
  - `0031-c64-6510-cpu-and-pla`: 6502/6510 CPU core, 64 KB RAM, and PLA memory-banking logic (BASIC/KERNAL/CHAR ROMs), verified with 6502 functional test suites in Amaranth.
  - `0032-c64-6510-pla-generated-verilog`: Dissecting 6502/6510 CPU execution FSM and PLA memory-banking Verilog.
  - `0033-c64-sid-synthesizer`: SID 6581/8580 digital synthesizer (3 voices, multi-waveform oscillators, ADSR envelopes, ring modulation/sync, resonant state-variable filter → I2S audio) in Amaranth.
  - `0034-c64-sid-generated-verilog`: Dissecting SID DSP oscillators, envelope generators, and resonant filter Verilog.
  - `0035-c64-vic-ii-video`: VIC-II video controller (character/bitmap modes, sprites, CPU/VIC bus sharing, badlines, raster interrupts → HDMI) in Amaranth.
  - `0036-c64-vic-ii-generated-verilog`: Dissecting VIC-II bus arbitration, raster-timing, and sprite-compositing Verilog.
  - `0037-c64-cia-keyboard-and-integration`: Dual CIA 6526 timers/I/O, keyboard input (UART/PS2/USB), `.prg` loading, and full C64 integration in Amaranth.
  - `0038-c64-full-core-generated-verilog`: Dissecting CIA timer/I/O, keyboard matrix, and full integrated C64 Verilog.
- **Phase 7 — Eurorack Audio-to-Video Synthesizer**
  - `0039-i2s-audio-input-and-eurorack-afe`: Eurorack ±5 V → 3 Vpp TL074 attenuator/AC-coupled front-end, `PCM1808` stereo I2S audio ADC receiver (MCLK derived from 27 MHz), and dual-port Block RAM ring buffer in Amaranth.
  - `0040-i2s-audio-input-generated-verilog`: Dissecting I2S bit-clock/word-select framing, serial-to-parallel shift registers, and ring-buffer Verilog.
  - `0041-spi-cv-adc-and-panel-controls`: DC-coupled TL074 ±5 V → 0–3.3 V scaling/offset + Schottky clamping front-end and `MCP3208` 8-channel 12-bit SPI ADC controller for Eurorack CV and front-panel pots in Amaranth.
  - `0042-spi-cv-adc-generated-verilog`: Dissecting multi-channel SPI polling FSM, channel-mux registers, and ADC sample latching Verilog.
  - `0043-audio-reactive-raster-and-scope`: Zero-crossing trigger, envelope follower DSP, and real-time 640×480 HDMI oscilloscope, X/Y Lissajous, and audio-modulated raster-bar video synthesis in Amaranth.
  - `0044-audio-reactive-raster-generated-verilog`: Dissecting trigger hysteresis, DSP envelope/scaling pipelines, and live raster pixel-generator Verilog.
  - `0045-video-feedback-and-eurorack-module`: On-chip 64 Mbit SDRAM framebuffer feedback/phosphor-persistence effects, Eurorack ±12 V / 5 V buck power distribution, and full standalone Eurorack audio-to-video module integration in Amaranth.
  - `0046-eurorack-video-synth-generated-verilog`: Dissecting the SDRAM feedback compositor and full Eurorack audio-to-video synthesizer Verilog.
- **Future Phase (Optional) — Reading, Dissecting & Porting Existing Verilog Cores**
  - Potential follow-on phase after the Amaranth-authored cores: applying the Verilog literacy built across all even-numbered lessons to read, simulate, port, and customize existing open-source Verilog cores on the Tang Nano 20K.
